About Me

An academic profile shaped by environmental science, interdisciplinary research, independent learning and an interest in evidence-informed environmental action.

Academic foundation

Environmental Science

I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Environmental Science at Swami Vivekanand Subharti University, Meerut, India. My academic interests connect ecological systems with climate science, environmental monitoring and sustainability practice.

Research orientation

From environmental evidence to decisions

My work and learning explore how scientific observations can inform environmental management and policy. I am particularly interested in climate mitigation, biodiversity, carbon storage, environmental governance and the use of geospatial tools to understand environmental change.

Technical interests

Methods and analytical tools

Remote sensing, Geographic Information Systems, environmental quality monitoring, biostatistics and carbon accounting form part of my developing technical toolkit.

Professional interests

Compliance and sustainability

I am developing practical familiarity with environmental compliance documentation, forest certification requirements, sustainability reporting and organizational approaches to environmental responsibility.

Areas of interest

Questions that guide my learning

Climate mitigation and carbon management

How can ecosystems, land management and credible measurement support climate mitigation?

Biodiversity and ecological resilience

How do habitat connectivity, restoration and biodiversity governance strengthen ecosystem resilience?

Remote sensing and GIS

How can Earth-observation data and spatial analysis improve environmental assessment and planning?

Environmental policy and compliance

How can scientific evidence, legal frameworks and organizational systems support better environmental decisions?

Personal learning

Philosophy and ecology

Alongside formal academic study, I have pursued sustained independent learning in Vedanta philosophy. This has informed a personal interest in the relationship between human conduct, nature and environmental responsibility.
